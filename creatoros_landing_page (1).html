FAQ
Common questions
Does it actually sound like me, or does it sound like AI?
CreatorOS trains a voice profile from your own writing samples and runs every output through a brand consistency check. If the tone drifts, it automatically revises before showing you. Most users' audiences can't tell the difference — we've had creators run for weeks without mentioning it.
Which platforms does the autopilot support?
Instagram (Reels + Feed), LinkedIn, X/Twitter, YouTube (Community posts + Shorts metadata), Pinterest, and Facebook Pages. TikTok autopublish is in beta — you can schedule the content and get a one-tap post reminder instead.
What video formats and lengths do you support?
MP4, MOV, and MP3 up to 4 hours. YouTube URLs of any length. Loom recordings. Podcast RSS feeds (auto-ingested daily). The longer the video, the more outputs and viral moment clips we can extract from it.
Can I edit the outputs before they go out?
Yes, always. Every output has an inline editor. You can edit, regenerate with new instructions, or reject it entirely. The autopilot only publishes outputs you've approved — you're always in control of what goes live.
Is my content stored or used to train AI models?
Your raw video files are stored temporarily (90-day TTL on S3) and are never used to train any model. Your brand voice data and generated content are stored privately and are never shared or used externally. You can delete all your data from Settings at any time.
